ARCHIVE POLICY
  1. Official information takes priority when an official source exists.
  2. Direct CBT2 observations and transcriptions are treated as beta data, not final release data.
  3. Community sources are cross-checked when possible. Conflicting or uncertain values remain unpublished.
  4. The archive does not invent missing stats, passive values, ranking scores, or player data.
02

MISSING DATA

Why some information is blank

Pre-release data

Silver Palace is still in development, so CBT2 values, terminology, equipment rules, and balance can change.

No reliable transcription

If a stat, passive, item name, or level value cannot be read or cross-checked confidently, it is left blank.

Conflicting beta records

When two beta/community records disagree, the archive does not silently choose one value. The field remains unpublished until better evidence is available.

No extrapolation

Missing Lv.MAX stats, refine values, or scaling are not calculated from lower-level data unless the game itself provides the formula.

03

CHARACTER RANKINGS

Why ranking scores are not published yet

FRAMEWORK READY

Score / Tier / Top % are intentionally empty.

The ranking interface is already prepared, but publishing scores now would require assumptions that cannot yet be tested consistently.

  • Characters have different intended combat roles.
  • Comparable reference builds are still incomplete.
  • Motive, Simulation, Epiphany, and balance values can change.
  • Reliable repeatable combat benchmarks are not yet available.

Rankings will be published only after the archive has a repeatable, role-aware scoring method and enough comparable combat data.

04

PLAYER LOOKUP

Why UID lookup does not work yet

WAITING FOR DATA SOURCE

The UID box is a future-facing placeholder.

A real Player Lookup needs a reliable public or official source that can return Silver Palace player profile and showcased-character data from a UID.

Until such a source exists, the archive will not simulate profiles or return invented player data. The planned future flow is:

UID → Player Profile → Showcase → Build Data → Score / Ranking
05

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is The Detective Archive official?

No. It is an independent fan-made database and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Elementa.

Are CBT2 numbers final?

No. They are pre-release observations and may change before launch.

Will blank data be filled later?

Yes, when a sufficiently reliable official or beta source becomes available.

Will Rankings eventually have real scores?

Yes. Score, Tier, and Top % are already supported by the interface, but they will remain empty until a defensible scoring method exists.

Will Player Lookup become like Enka.Network?

That is the long-term direction, but it depends on Silver Palace exposing a usable public or official player-data source.
