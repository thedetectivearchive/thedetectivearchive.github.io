CHARACTER RANKINGS

Why are Character Ranking scores still unpublished?

FRAMEWORK

Scores unpublished

No ranking scores are published yet. The table and filters are ready; verified values will be added when reliable combat data becomes available.

  • DPS, Break, Support and Survival characters are not judged by one identical priority.
  • Comparisons require a documented Motive, Simulation, Epiphany and stat baseline.
  • Damage and utility should be tested under consistent level, enemy and combat conditions.
  • A score stays unpublished when the available beta data is not strong enough to support it.

Scores will only be published after comparable combat data and a repeatable role-aware scoring method are available. The archive does not assign placeholder scores.

FAQ

Archive FAQ

Is The Detective Archive an official Silver Palace website?

No. The Detective Archive is an independent fan-made database and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Elementa.

Why can some stats, passives, or ranking scores be blank?

Silver Palace is still pre-release. When a value cannot be verified reliably, the archive leaves it blank instead of estimating or inventing it.

Are CBT2 numbers final?

No. Dichotomy CBT2 is a development build. Skills, stats, terminology, equipment, availability, and balance can change before release.

What is a Motive?

A Motive is equippable gear that provides character stats and a Motive Skill. Its Identity determines which characters can use it.

What is the difference between Simulation Implements and Epiphany?

Simulation uses four Implement slots that form equipment sets. Epiphany occupies a separate book slot and is the rollable-substat layer observed in CBT2.

What are Psyches?

Psyches are duplicate-copy character upgrades. The current CBT2 archive records six Psyche levels, P1 through P6, for each playable character entry.

Why are Character Ranking scores still unpublished?

Scores will only be published after comparable combat data and a repeatable role-aware scoring method are available. The archive does not assign placeholder scores.

Why does Player Lookup not work yet?

UID lookup requires a reliable public or official Silver Palace player-data source. The feature remains a placeholder until such a source exists.
